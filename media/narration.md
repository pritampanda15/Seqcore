## Tone notes

- Warm and unhurried; this is not a product ad.
- The turn at 1:03 is the point of the whole piece. Let it breathe — a full beat of silence before it.
- Do not oversell the speed numbers. They are real; let them be plain.
- Biopython is named rather than called "an older tool". It is the honest
  comparison and the more generous one; hedging the name would undercut the
  point the last third of the video is making.
